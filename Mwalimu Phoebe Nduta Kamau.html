Eulogy

Her life story, as written by the family.

Birth

The late Phoebe Nduta Kamau was born on 26th February 1971 in Njoro, Nakuru County. She was the last born of the late Rev. James Kamiru Nguuni and the late Florence Wambui Kamiru. She was a loving and adorable sister to Dr. Rebecca Wangari and Reuben Gikonyo; Rev. Ruth Wanjiku and Bishop Patrick Ichoho; Prof. John and Cynthia Gatiba; Peter and Peris Karanja; Naomi Nyambura Kamiru; Hannah Wanjiru and James Maina; Andrew Mbutha Kamiru; Grace Mukumi and John Muchiri; and Prof. Faith Njoki and Prof. Anthony Karanja.

She was a daughter-in-love to the late Stephen Njonge Njoroge and Dorothy Wanjiku Njonge. She was a sister-in-love to the late George Njoroge; Rahab Warigia and Christopher Mbugua; Peter and Peninah Waihenya; Loise Njango and Jamleck Mwangi; Samuel and Evelyn Njoroge; Benson Kuria; Susan Wangui; Francis and Grace Kinyuru; and the late Alex Waithaka.

Education

She started her education at Njoro Bondeni Nursery School and later joined Kenana Primary School. She then joined Loreto Matunda Girls in Kitale for her secondary education and later Ndururumo High School for her A-levels. She excelled and was admitted to Kenyatta University, where she graduated with a Bachelor of Education degree. Phoebe had enrolled for a Master's degree at JKUAT, where she had successfully completed her coursework and was developing a concept note that she was due to defend when she fell ill.

Work

Her first posting was Dondo Secondary School. She was later transferred to Kiambu Township, where she rose through the ranks to Principal. She then moved to Komothai Girls High School as Principal, and later to Gacharage Girls High School, where she served until her demise.

Marriage

Phoebe was married to Kenneth Kamau Njonge at St. Mark's ACK Westlands, Nairobi, in 1999. They were blessed with three sons: Mark Njonge Kamau (USA), Dr. James Kamiru Kamau and Manasseh Njoroge Kamau (USA).

Church

Phoebe was baptised and confirmed at ACK Emmanuel Church, Njoro. She was a member of St. James Cathedral Kiambu, Diocese of Mt. Kenya South, where she served in various capacities and was a member of the Mothers' Union.

Sickness

Phoebe started feeling unwell in December 2025 and sought treatment in various hospitals, where the necessary treatment was administered. On Saturday 7th November 2026 her condition worsened and she was admitted to The Nairobi Hospital. Sadly, on Sunday morning, 8th November 2026, at 8:30 a.m., she went to rest while undergoing treatment.

“Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.”PSALM 116:15

Guciarwo

Mwendwo ni-iri Phoebe Nduta Kamau aciarirwo mweri 26th February 1971 itura ini ria Njoro, Nakuru County ari mwana wa muico wa Mutiga-iri mutungatiri James Kamiru Nguuni na mwendwo ni-iri Florence Wambui Kamiru. Arari mwendwa wa hakuhi na mwari wa nyina na Dr. Rebecca Wangari na Reuben Gikonyo, Rev. Ruth Wanjiku na Bishop Patrick Ichoho, Prof. John na Cynthia Gatiba, Peter na Peris Karanja, Naomi Nyambura kamiru, Hannah Wanjiru na James Maina, Andrew Mbutha Kamiru, Grace Mukami na John Muchiri na Prof. Faith Njoki na Prof. Antony Karanja.

Arari mwari wa mutiga-iri Stephen Njonge Njoroge na Dorothy Wanjiku Njonge. Arari mwari wa nyina na mutiga-iri George Njoroge, Rahab Warigia na Christopher Mbugua, Peter na Peninah Waihenya, Loise Njango na Jamleck Mwangi, Samuel na Evelyn Njoroge, Benson Kuria, Susan Wangui, Francis na grace Kinyuru na mutiga-iri Alex Waithaka.

Githomo

Ambiriirie thukuru Njoro Bondeni Nursery School na thutha ucio akiingira thukuru wa Kenana Primary School. Ni acokire akiingira cukuru wa Loreto Matunda Girls Secondary School kuria Kitale niundu wa githomo gia secondary. Niacokire agithii Ndururumo High School niundu wa githomo kia A-Level (kana Advanced Level). Niahitukire na agitwo Kenyatta University guthomera Degree ya Bachelor of Education (kana walimu). Phoebe nieyandikithitie guthomera Masters Degree kuria JKUAT kuria ararikitie course work kana wira wa kirathi no atanarikiriria akirwara.

Wira

Mwendwo ni-iri Phoebe niambiriirie wira wa walimu thukuru-ini wa Dondo Secondary School agicoka agicenjio agithie Kiambu township Secondary kuria ahaicirio ngathi o kinya agatuika mwalimu munene kana principal. Niacenjirio thukuru agithie Komothai Girls High School ee principal. Thutha ucio niathire Gacharage Girls ari oo principal nginya kuhuruka gwake.

Kihiko

Mwendwo ni-iri nimahikanirie na mwendwa wake Kenneth Kamau Njoroge kanitha-ini wa St. Mark ACK Westlands Nairobi mwaka –ini wa 1999. Nimarathimirwo na anake atatu nio; Mark Njonge Kamau (U.S.A), Dr. James Kamiru Kamau na Manasseh Njoroge (U.S.A).

Kanitha

Phoebe niabatithirio kanitha-ini wa ACK Emmanuel Church Njoro, na thutha ucio akiigirirwo moko kanitha-ini o ucio. Niakoretwo ari murumiriri wa St. James Cathedral Kiambu diocese of Mt. Kenya South kuria atungatite na ikiro ngurani. Akoretwo ari wa Mothers Union.

Ndwari

Phoebe niambiriirie guthinio ni mwiri December 2025. Niacaririe urigiti mathibitari ngurani kuria aheirwo urigitani uria wagiriire. Kuri Jumamothi mweri 7/11/2026, niakomire thibitari wa Nairobi Hospital na akihurukio ni mwathani mweri 8/11/2026 o akirigitagwo.

“Maithoini ma Ngai kihuruko kia mwitikia ni kiega.”THABURI 116:15 (A)
